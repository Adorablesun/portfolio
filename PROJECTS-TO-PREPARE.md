# Ong Yu Yang — portfolio project plan

Start with your best existing assignments and personal work. Aim for 4–6 substantial projects; the school timeline provides background, while finished work demonstrates your abilities.

## Suggested first collection

1. **Motion graphics / 3D piece.** A 20–45 second title sequence, product film, or visual explainer. Include the brief, storyboard, style frames, final video, and a breakdown showing your own modelling, animation, or compositing contribution.
2. **Brand identity system.** A real or clearly labelled concept brand with a logo, type and colour system, posters/social applications, and a short animated identity. Explain the audience and why the visual choices fit.
3. **Short film / video-editing project.** Select a 1–3 minute sequence with strong pacing, colour grading, sound, and visual effects. For group work, credit the team and clearly state which shots or tasks were yours.
4. **Interactive multimedia experience.** A clickable Figma prototype, interactive installation concept, or web experience demonstrating navigation and motion. A working prototype is enough; you do not need to present yourself as a software developer.
5. **Strongest university or internship case study.** Use an actual project with a clear brief, your contribution, constraints, deliverables, and feedback. Include ZTE work only if it is approved for public sharing; otherwise use a personal or academic project.

Then edit a **45–60 second showreel** from the strongest finished work. Open with your strongest shots; the reel introduces the case studies rather than replacing them.

These durations and categories are tailored suggestions, not mandatory industry rules. Existing excellent work is more valuable than filling every category.

## Prepare these files for each project

- Title, date, project type (academic/personal/client/internship), and a one-sentence brief.
- Your exact role, software/tools, collaborators and credits.
- One landscape cover image and 3–6 useful process or final images.
- Final video with playback controls and captions/transcript when needed, or a prototype link.
- 150–300 words covering the problem, important choices, outcome and reflection.
- Actual feedback or measured results where available; do not invent metrics.
